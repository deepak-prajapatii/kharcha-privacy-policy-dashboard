# Kharcha Privacy Policy

The privacy policy for **Kharcha**, the Android expense tracker that records spends from bank and UPI notifications on your phone.

**Live page:** https://deepak-prajapatii.github.io/kharcha-privacy/

This URL is the privacy policy link in the Google Play Console.

## Updating the policy

1. Edit `index.html`. It is a single self-contained page: styles and icons are inline, and only the Plus Jakarta Sans font loads from Google Fonts.
2. Change the effective date in the hero chip (`EFFECTIVE …`).
3. Commit and push to `main`. GitHub Pages republishes in a minute or two.

Update the policy **before** shipping anything that changes what leaves the phone, such as Cloud assist or Google Drive sync.
