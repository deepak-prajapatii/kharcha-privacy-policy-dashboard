1Information the app reads

Bank and UPI notificationsWith notification access on, Kharcha looks at incoming notifications to find bank, card and UPI alerts. From a transaction alert it takes the amount, merchant or payee, date, the last 4 digits of the account and the reference number, and keeps the original alert text so you can see where each entry came from. Notifications that are not transactions are ignored and not stored. If a bank alert cannot be read, only a count is kept, never its text.
Receipt scansWhen you choose to scan a receipt, the image is read on your phone. The photo is never saved or uploaded. The text read from it is saved with the expense, on your phone.
What you enterExpenses you add yourself, category changes and the budgets you set.

2Where your information is stored

Transactions, budgets and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Kharcha opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so none of it is copied to a server.

If you use Export as CSV, a file is saved wherever you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

3Information that leaves your phone

Crash reports Firebase CrashlyticsIf the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report is sent so it can be fixed: device model, Android version, app version, a random installation ID, the stack trace and short error messages. Notification text, amounts and merchant names are never included. See Firebase privacy information.
Text recognition model Google Play servicesReceipt scanning uses Google ML Kit. Its model is downloaded and managed by Google Play services; the reading itself happens on your phone.

5Permissions

Notification accessTo read bank and UPI alerts and record spends automatically. Turn it off any time in Android Settings.
CameraOnly when you scan a receipt.
NotificationsTo warn you when a budget is nearly used up or exceeded.
Fingerprint or screen lockOnly if you turn on App lock. Android checks your fingerprint or face; the app never receives that data.

6Sharing and selling

Your personal and financial information is not sold, rented or shared. The app has no advertising and uses no advertising identifiers. The only third party that receives any data is Google, as the crash reporting provider described above.

7Keeping and deleting your data

You are in controlDelete any transaction inside the app. Clear everything from Android Settings › Apps › Kharcha › Storage › Clear storage. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone.
Crash reportsKept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.

There is no account or server copy of your transactions, so they cannot be seen or recovered by anyone but you.

8Security

Your data lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. You can turn on App lock (fingerprint or screen lock) and Hide the app preview, which blanks Kharcha in recent apps and blocks screenshots. Crash reports are encrypted in transit.

9Children

Kharcha is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. No information is knowingly collected from children.

10Changes to this policy

Any update will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes, such as turning on a feature that sends data off your phone, will also be shown in the app.
